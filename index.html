Read, learn and chant in English — with meaning, guided learning, and your own private japa counter.

This site is offered in devotion to Shri Hanuman and Shri Ram. It holds the English transliteration and plain-English meaning of the Ram Stuti, Hanuman Chalisa and Bajrang Baan, so that newcomers and long-time devotees alike can read along, learn each verse line by line with guided audio, and keep a private count of how many times they've completed each recitation (japa) — tracked only in your own browser, never shared anywhere.

Frequently asked

What is the Hanuman Chalisa?

A devotional hymn of forty verses, composed by the poet-saint Tulsidas, praising the strength, wisdom and devotion of Shri Hanuman.

What is Bajrang Baan?

"The arrow of Bajrang Bali" — a devotional prayer invoking Hanuman's protection, traditionally recited for courage in times of hardship or fear.

What is Ram Stuti?

"Shri Ramchandra Kripalu Bhajman" — a hymn by Tulsidas in praise of Shri Ram's compassion and divine form, often recited during Ram aarti.
